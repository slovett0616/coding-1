Mechanics: A simple straight foward infinite side scroller where you click/tap to make a yellow bird fly while trying to get
the highest score you can. Its the only gameplay loop and the only thing you can do is fly.

Dynamics: You have to dodge randomly generated green pipes. If you get hit by one of the pipes you will have to start over completely.

Asthetics: Mainly an 8-bit look to appeal to kids. Its bright and vibrant and overall has a simple art style.


1. I plan to make a open world rpg set in the Heian era. Where you play as a samurai traveling to different dojo's trying to get stronger.

2. Players will complete side quests, travel to different dojo's and defeat enemies and bosses.

3. If a player completes a main quest or side quest they will be rewarded with rewards such as skill points or money. If its at
a certain point in a main quest then they might learn a new skill.

4. Depending on if the player fails or succeeds a death count system will effect the weather. If you die too many times, it will
begin to rain and slowly transform into a storm. If you dont die as much, the sky will be full of color and the environment will 
feel more alive.

5. Games im taking inspiration from are ghost of tsushima, sekiro, and elden ring.
