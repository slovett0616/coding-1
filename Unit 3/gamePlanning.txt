Mach-

1. A 2d momentum based high speed platformer where you perform combos to keep your speed and even move faster. 
The combo meter depends on how fast you go. Landing on enemies will also maintain the speed and build combo meter. To complete levels
you have to have a certain amount of score along with moving at a certain speed. 


2. You can move with: w a s d or arrows keys to move around. Pressing Z will allow you to jump and pressing X will allow you to perform tricks. 
Pressing X has variants, clicking X on the ground will allow you to roll down slopes to build up speed and kill enemies, 
and clicking X in the air will allow you to do air tricks. Pressing C will allow you to do a basic attack, you can combine this with X in the air to kill enemies while
airborne.


3. 2 objcets I will need in the game are maps that fit the games mechanics and the character you play as. The map will include: enemies, item boxes, and slopes.
The character you play as will include a speed meter, abilities, and jump mechanic.

Replayability- achievements, unlocking abilities, and mastering the mechanics to complete levels as fast
as possible for more rewards.

Inspirations- Sonic the hedgehog, Mario, Fancy pants adventures.
