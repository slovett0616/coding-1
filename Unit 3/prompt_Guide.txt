1. A momentum based 2d running game where the player is tasked to complete multiple levels and travel through worlds to get to harder levels until they 
reach the end of the game. What makes the game challanging and fun is the level design that has multiple pathways that allow for 
replayability in previous levels, and slopes that builds momentum to have you running as fast as you possibly can.
You can also unlock a variety of skills that change the way you play these levels. 


2. There will be a menu consisting of a settings button that lets you tweak visual or keybinds and a play button that actually
lets you enter the game.


3. The game objects that will be in the game are the player model, the enemies, and a end flag to complete a level. The Player model and enemies
would look like stick figures, similar to a game called "Fancy Pants Adventures". The end flag


4. The player will only be allowed to move right, and cannot move left. As a player runs, they will passivley gain speed, but if 
theyre hit by an enemy they will loose that speed. Killing enemies by jumping on them, rolling into them, or attacking them 
will give them score and maintain their speed. Using a Trick on the ground will allow you to roll, but using it in the air after
jumping off of a ledge or jumping off of an enemy will allow you to do an air trick for more score.
Player control scheme: 
 D/right arrow: move right
 Z: Jump
 X: Roll/AirTricks
 C: Attack


5. There will be gaps in the map that act as voids that can instantly kill you if you fall into them.
Some enemy objects will have 3 modes, passive, fear, and attack mode. Passive mode is the mode theyre naturally in, they do not 
attack in this mode and walk around. Attack mode is the mode they enter whenever you are spotted, they will shoot bullets at
you, or rush towards you depending on the enemy type. Fear mode is the mode they enter if they are already in attack mode and they spot
you killing another enemy. However, its a 5% chance this can happen and the chance can be increased as you progress through
the game.


6. To win the game, you have to fill out a level requirement, wether thats killing a certain amount of enemies, getting enough score, 
or completing a level while running at a certain speed. To lose you either have to fail the level requirement and get to the end of the stage,
or die to an enemy.
